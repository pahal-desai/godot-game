# Dont touch the red!

## what is this?
this is a godot game that is made on godot (idk how to explain. but i'll try my best :D)

## how to play??
the main menu will appear first, then there are only 2 games you can play. (since i'm a beginner at coding.)

### game 1 - dont touch the red
you control a green square and you have to avoid the red squares. (there are also yellow coins, collect them to increase your score!)
the longer you survive, the faster the enemies get and the more frequently they spawn.

### game 2 - smash the green
you click the green squares that appear randomly on the screen. each time you click a green square, you get 1 point. if you click on the red square, you lose!

## Controls
WASD or arrow keys to move. --> for game 1
mouse click to click on the squares. --> for game 2

## the end!
if you win, you'll be taken to a winner screen. if you lose, you'll be taken to a game over screen.
